## About Ezhunai

Ezhunai is an initiative designed to bring curious people together to exchange knowledge, explore interdisciplinary interests, develop practical skills, and build ideas collaboratively.

We participate as equals: someone may teach a concept they have practiced for years while simultaneously learning an entirely new craft from another member.

---

## Etymology & Meaning

| Tamil Root | Transliteration | Significance |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **எழு** | *Ezhu* | To rise, awaken, grow, or advance forward |
| **இணை** | *Inai* | To connect, unite, or come together |
| **எழுநை** | **Ezhunai** | **Rise Together** |

> *\"கற்றது கைமண் அளவு, கல்லாதது உலகளவு\"*  
> *(What we have learned is like a handful of sand; what we have yet to learn is as vast as the world).*

## Disciplines Explored

- **Technology & Programming**: Open source development, web engineering, systems, and tooling.
- **Design & Visual Identity**: Graphic design, UI/UX, typography, illustration, and creative experiments.
- **Writing & Communication**: One-minute talks, technical writing, storytelling, and public speaking.
- **Business & Problem Solving**: Product thinking, entrepreneurship, planning, and practical solutions.
- **Science & Lifelong Learning**: Curiosity-driven questions, research, and cross-disciplinary exploration.

---

## Core Philosophy

- **Peer-to-Peer**: We participate as equals with no hierarchical barriers.
- **Curiosity Over Perfection**: Sincere exploration matters more than pretending to have all the answers.
- **Tamil Roots, Global Circle**: Grounded in Tamil identity while welcoming participants worldwide.
- **Privacy First**: Free participation without forced personal data collection.
