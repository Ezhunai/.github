## 🌿 About Ezhunai

Ezhunai is an initiative designed to bring curious people together to learn from one another, exchange knowledge, explore interests, develop skills, and collaborate on ideas.

Rooted in Tamil identity while remaining open to the world, Ezhunai welcomes beginners, creative explorers, builders, and lifelong learners across diverse disciplines:
- 💻 **Technology & Programming**: Open source, web development, digital tools
- 🎨 **Art & Design**: Visual identity, UI/UX, illustration, creative experiments
- 🗣️ **Writing & Communication**: 1-minute talks, storytelling, public speaking practice
- 💡 **Business & Problem Solving**: Entrepreneurship, product building, practical problem-solving
- 🔬 **Science & Exploration**: Curiosity-driven learning across diverse subjects

## 📖 The Name & Meaning

| Tamil | Transliteration | Meaning |
| :---: | :---: | :--- |
| **எழு** | *Ezhu* | To rise, grow, or move forward |
| **இணை** | *Inai* | To connect, unite, or come together |
| **எழுநை** | **Ezhunai** | **Rise Together** |

> *\"கற்றது கைமண் அளவு, கல்லாதது உலகளவு\"*  
> *(What we have learned is like a handful of sand; what we have yet to learn is like the size of the world).*

## 🤝 Community Philosophy

- **Peer-to-Peer**: We participate as equals. Everyone can be both a teacher and a learner.
- **Curiosity Over Perfection**: Questions and exploration matter more than pretending to know everything.
- **Tamil Roots, Global Circle**: Celebrating cultural roots without exclusionary boundaries.
- **Privacy First**: Free participation without forced personal data collection.
